# 🎓 FYPHelpDesk

> **An AI-Powered Final Year Project (FYP) Supervisor Recommendation System for Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU).**

FYPHelpDesk is a modern academic web platform designed to simplify the Final Year Project (FYP) supervisor selection process for students at **Allama Iqbal Open University (AIOU)**. The platform intelligently recommends supervisors based on a student's selected project domain, faculty expertise, and research specialization, enabling students to make informed decisions when choosing an FYP supervisor.

Traditionally, students rely on seniors, classmates, or word-of-mouth to understand which faculty member is best suited for a particular project domain. FYPHelpDesk aims to replace this uncertainty with a structured, transparent, and user-friendly recommendation system.

---

## 📖 Overview

Selecting the right supervisor is one of the most important decisions during a student's undergraduate journey. Different faculty members possess expertise in different research areas such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Software Engineering, Cyber Security, Data Science, Internet of Things (IoT), Robotics, Networking, and many others.

FYPHelpDesk centralizes this information into a single platform, allowing students to:

- Discover faculty members and their areas of expertise
- Search supervisors by research domain
- Receive intelligent supervisor recommendations
- Explore suitable project domains
- Compare multiple supervisors
- Make data-informed supervisor selections
