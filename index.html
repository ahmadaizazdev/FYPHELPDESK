About

About the System

How the AIOU FYP Supervisor Recommendation System works.

Our Mission

To simplify supervisor selection for FYP students at Allama Iqbal Open University by providing intelligent, data‑driven recommendations based on project domains and specialization.

How It Works

Choose your domain from the list of cards. The system then shows you all supervisors who are experts in that domain. You can click any supervisor to see their full profile, research areas, and suggested niches.

For Students

BS Computer Science students can find supervisors who specialize in their area of interest, reducing confusion and making selection efficient.

Smart Matching

Our algorithm prioritizes supervisors based on your chosen domain, considering primary and secondary expertise, research areas, and project affinity.
